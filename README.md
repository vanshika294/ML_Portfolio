## 🧠 Philosophy & Learning Approach

This repository documents my structured and intentional journey into Machine Learning. I am beginning with fundamentals, small experiments, and guided exploration rather than fully polished projects. The focus is on asking questions, testing ideas, and understanding concepts step by step, especially when outcomes are not immediately clear.

- **Understanding the "Why":** I focus on understanding how models behave, why predictions change, and what happens behind the scenes during training and evaluation.

- **Connecting Theory & Code:** Every experiment connects theory, concepts, and code into one unified understanding, treating mistakes and unexpected results as key learning moments.

- **Depth Over Speed:** Understanding matters more than rushing. Consistency matters more than intensity.
